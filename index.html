Patty’s Dream Houses

I have a big and beautiful house. There three rooms in it. My bedroom is my favourite place. There a comfortable bed and a big white wardrobe for my clothes. There a blue carpet on the floor and a yellow lamp on the desk. There many books on the bookshelf, and there funny paintings on the wall. Next to the bed, there a large mirror. I also have a small bathroom. There a white bath, a toilet and a washbasin in it.

There a big kitchen and a living room. In the kitchen, there a new cooker, a fridge, a sink and a brown table with four chairs. The living room is very bright. There two windows and a long sofa with an armchair. There a small coffee table in front of the sofa. When you enter the house, there a small hall. I love my beautiful house.
